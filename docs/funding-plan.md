## Executive Summary

SafeChat is open-source crisis safety infrastructure for AI chat systems. It detects distress signals locally (zero data collection), geo-locates users without GPS, and connects them to verified professional crisis resources across 34 countries. It is a routing layer, not a diagnostic tool.

This plan identifies tiered funding opportunities aligned with SafeChat's positioning as open-source digital public infrastructure for AI safety and suicide prevention.

## Licensing Consideration

SafeChat uses BSL-1.1 (Business Source License), which is source-available but **not OSI-approved**. Some funders (Sovereign Tech Fund, NLnet, DPGA) prefer or require OSI-approved licenses.

**Options:**
1. **Apply as-is** — BSL-1.1 is functionally open for most use cases. Some funders accept it. Explain the change date (MPL 2.0 on 2029-01-01).
2. **Dual license** — Add an OSI-approved license (Apache 2.0 or MPL 2.0) alongside BSL-1.1 for the grant-funded portions.
3. **Accelerate change date** — Move the MPL 2.0 change date earlier (e.g., 2027) to demonstrate open-source commitment.

This is a strategic decision with commercial implications. Consider before applying to license-strict funders.

## Key Pitch Points (For All Applications)

1. **Problem:** AI companion chatbots are being used by millions, particularly young people. VERA-MH research (2026) found significant gaps in how major AI systems respond to suicidal ideation. New York's AI Companion Law and FTC inquiries signal regulatory urgency.

2. **Solution:** SafeChat is a routing layer — it detects textual distress signals and connects users to verified professional crisis resources. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional services.

3. **Differentiators:** Zero data collection (all local), works offline (PWA), 34 countries, 420 automated tests, Samaritans-compliant, BSL-1.1 licensed, drop-in integration (one script tag).

4. **Traction:** Live at rob-e-graham.github.io/safechat. 100+ verified helplines. Configurable Shield class with 6 presets for different deployment contexts. Active CHANGELOG with public git history documenting every improvement.

5. **Sustainability:** FAMTEC commercial licensing for entities over $100K revenue. Open-source core remains free for personal, research, education, nonprofit, and small commercial use.
