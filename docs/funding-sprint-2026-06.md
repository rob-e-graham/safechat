### Best Framing

SafeChat is not a clinical intervention and does not assess suicide risk. It is a privacy-preserving routing layer that detects textual distress signals in AI chat interfaces and connects users to verified professional crisis resources.

The grant should fund:

- independent review of detection patterns
- lived-experience and service-provider feedback on resource presentation
- formal helpline data verification workflow
- implementation study with one or more chat-based systems
- false-positive and false-negative evaluation protocol
- accessibility and cultural safety review

### Proposed Project Title

**Privacy-Preserving Crisis Routing for AI Chat Systems: Evaluation and Implementation of SafeChat**

### Draft Abstract

AI chat systems are increasingly used for emotionally vulnerable conversations, yet many lack reliable mechanisms for responding when users disclose distress, self-harm, or suicidal ideation. SafeChat is a local-first crisis routing layer that detects textual distress signals on-device and connects users to verified professional crisis resources without collecting personal data or requiring location permissions. This project will evaluate SafeChat's detection patterns, resource presentation, and implementation pathway in partnership with suicide prevention researchers, service providers, and people with lived experience. The project will not develop or test a diagnostic tool; it will assess a privacy-preserving referral and routing mechanism intended to complement, not replace, professional crisis services and local emergency procedures.

**Timing:** Rolling concept note
